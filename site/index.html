Your store's month-end review, in seconds

Drop in a monthly P&L CSV. Get margins, what moved profit vs last month, unit economics, cash warnings and a CFO-style write-up. Nothing is uploaded; it all runs in your browser.

Download CSV template

1. Export

Columns: month, gross_sales, cogs. Optional: discounts, refunds, shipping, payment_fees, ad_spend, payroll, apps_software, other_opex, orders, ending_cash, ending_inventory. Common names like "revenue" or "marketing" work too.

2. Review

P&L waterfall, month-on-month variance and an EBITDA bridge, plus margin, MER, contribution per order, inventory days and runway.

3. Act

Rule-based flags and commentary for free. Optionally add your own OpenAI or Anthropic key for an AI CFO note with next-month actions.

Built by Siddharth Kumar, ex-Amazon finance partner who runs P&Ls for ~50 Shopify stores. Based on the AI Financial Coach Agent from Shubhamsaboo/awesome-llm-apps.
